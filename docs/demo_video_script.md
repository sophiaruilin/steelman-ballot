# Demo video script (about 2½ minutes)

**Setup before recording**
- Open https://steelman-ballot.onrender.com about a minute before you record, so the server is awake. (The keep-awake ping usually handles this.)
- Use a phone-sized browser window (in Chrome or Edge: F12 → device toolbar → iPhone 12/13), or record your actual phone screen.
- Clear the last ballot first: if "Show my last ballot" appears, tap **Forget it**.
- Zoom to 100%, hide bookmarks, and close other tabs.
- Windows: **Win + Alt + R** starts and stops a recording with the Xbox Game Bar. Mac: **Shift + Cmd + 5**.

| Time | On screen | What to say (about 330 words in total) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Home page, top | "This is Steelman Ballot. Ballot measures are real laws, but the official guide for California's 14 propositions runs about two and a half hours of reading, and most of what voters hear is the other side's weakest argument." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Scroll slowly through **Steelman vs. strawman** | "So instead of a strawman, we give voters a steelman: each side at its strongest, stated so well that its own supporters would sign it. For both sides, every time." |
| 0:30–0:45 | Scroll back up and type **1315 10th St, Sacramento, CA**; point at **✓ Ready**; tap **See my ballot** | "You type an address or a ZIP code. It's used once and never stored." |
| 0:45–1:00 | Progress steps, then **Your ballot in 60 seconds** | "The app finds your districts, reads the official voter guide, and here's my ballot: 14 measures and 8 races, with the address it matched so I can check it." |
| 1:00–1:35 | Open **Prop 39**: the two sides → "Explore reasoning" → **Where they really disagree** → **What the evidence says** | "Each card puts the strongest case against on the left and the strongest case for on the right, then where they really disagree, and the evidence, each line labeled fact or estimate, with a source. One AI wrote this card. A second, different AI played a committed supporter of each side and scored it. On the first try, it gave the Yes side 3 out of 5 for missing an argument, so the card was rewritten until both sides scored 4." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Scroll to **What would change**, then **How to vote on this** and **Next →** | "Then exactly what would change if it passes, how voting on it works, and a link straight to the next measure. It never tells you how to vote." |
| 1:50–2:10 | Switch to **中文**; tap **WeChat** to show the share image; then **ES** | "It's in Spanish and Chinese too, and sharing always sends both sides: here's a WeChat image friends can scan." |
| 2:10–2:30 | **How it works** page: the 1–5 scale, then the accuracy step | "Graded against California's nonpartisan Legislative Analyst, 100% of claims were backed by the official guide. Each card costs about 30 cents to research once, and every voter after that is free. Think more deeply. Vote more thoughtfully." |

**Tips**
- Speak a little slower than feels natural, and pause one second after each tap.
- If a scroll goes wrong, keep going; you can trim it later in the Photos app (Windows) or QuickTime (Mac).
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or to Canvas directly) and put the link in the report's title block and the README.
