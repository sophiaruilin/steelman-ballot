# Session script (about 20 minutes)

Use two California measures that have a clear official answer key:
- **Measure X = Prop 3** (making higher income tax rates permanent)
- **Measure Y = Prop 39** (voter ID and citizenship checks)

| Participant | First (by hand) | Then (with the app) |
|---|---|---|
| P1, P3, P5, P7 | Prop 3 by hand | Prop 39 with the app |
| P2, P4, P6, P8 | Prop 39 by hand | Prop 3 with the app |

(This rotation balances which measure and which method comes first.)

---

## 0. Intro (1 min)
> "Thanks for helping. I'm testing a ballot guide, not you. There are no wrong answers, and honest criticism helps most.
> I'll time a couple of short tasks. Please think out loud if you can. Your answers are anonymous."

Ask the **background** questions (questions.md, section 0).

## 1. By hand (max 6 min, timed)
> "Imagine you're voting on [measure]. Using whatever you'd normally use (Google, news, the official guide, anything),
> find out enough to feel ready to vote. Tell me when you feel ready."

- Start the timer. Stop when they say "ready" or at **6:00**. Record minutes.
- Then ask the **3 comprehension questions** for that measure (questions.md, section 1).
- Ask: "How confident do you feel about how you'd vote?" (1–5)

## 2. With the app (max 6 min, timed)
> "Now open steelman-ballot.onrender.com on your phone. Type a California address (any is fine, e.g. *1315 10th St, Sacramento, CA* or the ZIP *95814*), then open [the other measure].
> Same thing: tell me when you feel ready to vote."

- Timer, comprehension questions for that measure, confidence (1–5).
- **Don't help.** Note anything they tap that doesn't work, or where they hesitate.

## 3. Fairness (2 min), on the card they just used
Fairness questions (section 2). Ask them to look at both sides before answering.

## 4. Ease of use (1 min)
UMUX-Lite (section 3).

## 5. Share test (2 min)
> "Please share this card with yourself, or with me, on WhatsApp or WeChat, whichever you use, and open the link."

Record what happened (section 4). If someone uses WeChat, ask them to try the QR code too.

## 6. Language (optional, 2 min)
If they read Spanish or Chinese: switch the language at the top (ES / 中文), open a card, and answer section 5.

## 7. Open questions (3 min)
Section 6. Then: "Anything else?" Thank them.

---

### Facilitator notes to capture (in the results sheet "notes" column)
- Where they got stuck or confused (exact words if possible)
- Anything they said was surprising, unfair, or especially helpful
- Bugs: what device, what they tapped, what happened
